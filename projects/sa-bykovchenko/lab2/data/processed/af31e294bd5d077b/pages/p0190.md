### 9.13.3 Reaction Control System Propellant

The increased hover time for lunar landing resulted in a deviation
of over 10 percent in the reaction control system propellant usage, as

compared with the preflight predictions. Propellant consumption was calculated from telemetered helium tank pressure histories using the relationships between pressure, volume, and temperature. The mixture ratio
was assumed to be 1.94 for the calculations.

### 9.13.4 Oxygen

because oxygen leak rate from the cabin was less than the specification
value. The actual rate was 0.05 lb/hr, as compared with the specification
