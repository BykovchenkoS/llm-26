The performance of the communication system during the ascent and rendezvous phases was nominal except for a 15-second loss of downlink phase lock at ascent engine ignition. The data indicate this loss can be attributed to rapid phase perturbations caused by transmission through the ascent engine plume. During future Apollo missions, a wider carrier tracking loop bandwidth will be selected by the Network stations prior to powered ascent. This change will minimize the possibility of loss of lock due to rapid phase perturbations.
