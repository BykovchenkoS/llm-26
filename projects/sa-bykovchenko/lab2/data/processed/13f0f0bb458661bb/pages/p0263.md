# PDF page 263 — BIOMEDICAL EVALUATION

[Original PDF, page 263](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/apollo50th/pdf/A11_MissionReport.pdf#page=263)

The personnel in quarantine and in the crew reception area of the Lunar Receiving Laboratory were approved for release from quarantine on August 10, 1969.

Following decontamination using formaldehyde, the interior of the command module and the ground servicing equipment utilized in the decontamination procedures were approved for release from quarantine on August 10, 1969.

The samples of lunar material and other items stored in the biological isolation containers in the Lunar Receiving Laboratory are scheduled for release to principal scientific investigators in September 1969.
