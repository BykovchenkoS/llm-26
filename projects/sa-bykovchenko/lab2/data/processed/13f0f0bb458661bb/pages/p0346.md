APPENDIX C -POSTFLIGHT TESTING

The command module arrived at the Lunar Receiving Laboratory, Houston, Texas, on July 30, 1969, after reaction control system deactivation and pyrotechnic safing in Hawaii. After decontamination and at the end of the quarantine period, the command module was shipped to the contractor's facility in Downey, California, on August 14. Postflight testing and inspection of the command module for evaluation of the inflight performance and investigation of the flight irregularities were conducted at the contractor's and vendor's facilities and at the Manned Spacecraft Center in accordance with approved Apollo Spacecraft Hardware Utilization Requests (ASHUR's) . The tests performed as a result of in flight problems are described in table G-I and discussed in the appropriate systems performance sections of this report. Tests being conducted for other purposes in accordance with other ASHUR's and the basic contract are not included.
